Privacy Policy

Information about how this website may handle information provided by visitors.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

City Boy Movement – Ibaji Branch respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy explains the general approach to information that may be provided or collected when using the website.

By using this website, visitors acknowledge this policy and the information described in it.

2. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you interact with the website, information that you voluntarily provide may include:

  • Your name or other contact details.
  • Information submitted when contacting the branch.
  • Information provided when making membership enquiries.
  • Messages or enquiries sent through available contact channels.

The website may also receive basic technical information through normal web hosting or browser processes.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information provided by visitors may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Providing requested information.
  • Communicating about activities or programmes.
  • Managing website-related enquiries.
  • Improving the website and visitor experience.

4. Information Sharing

Personal information should not be publicly displayed on this website without an appropriate reason or authorization.

Where information must be shared with a service provider or other party for website-related purposes, reasonable steps should be taken to limit the information shared to what is necessary.

5. External Links

This website may contain links to external websites, including social media and communication platforms.

External websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Visitors should review those policies when using external services.

6. Website Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet-based system can guarantee complete security.

Visitors should avoid submitting sensitive personal or financial information through unsecured channels.

7. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the website, its services or its information practices.

The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For questions relating to this Privacy Policy or the website, please use the official contact information available on the Contact page.
