Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

City Boy Movement – Ibaji Branch respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, information may include:

  • Your name and contact information when you voluntarily submit a form.
  • Your community, ward or other information you choose to provide.
  • Information contained in messages sent through our contact channels.
  • Basic technical information that may be provided by your browser or hosting service.

2. Membership Information

If you complete the membership registration form, the information you provide may be used for membership administration, communication, activities and related organizational purposes.

3. How Information Is Used

Information voluntarily provided through this website may be used to respond to enquiries, manage registrations, provide information about activities and programmes, and maintain the operation of the website.

4. Information Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information submitted through this website. Information may only be shared where necessary for legitimate organizational purposes, with service providers supporting the website, or where required by applicable law.

5. Website Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

This website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Some website features may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to provide functionality. Information stored locally by your browser remains subject to the settings and controls of your device and browser.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent or authorization.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the use of information on this website, please use the contact options provided on the website.
